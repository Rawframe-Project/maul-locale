# Where mlocgen's patterns differ from ICU4C 79's (test/skeletons_icu.tsv)
# on purpose, with mlocgen's pattern. Both read CLDR 49; ICU's data build
# resolves two things otherwise than CLDR's inheritance (UTS #35, root's
# aliases resolved from the locale asked for), which mlocgen follows:
# - The date-time glue of a calendar other than the Gregorian: CLDR gives
#   the calendar's own atTime glue, through root's alias to its standard
#   one ("{1} {0}" for many); ICU's data has no atTime glue for those
#   calendars and takes the Gregorian one ("{1}, {0}" for many).
# - The time formats of the Chinese and Dangi calendars: root aliases
#   them to the locale's Gregorian ones ("HH.mm" in da); ICU takes root's
#   own Chinese formats ("HH:mm").
# CLDR's own skeletons*.tsv joins with the calendar's glue as mlocgen does.
# locale, calendar, skeleton, mlocgen's pattern.
